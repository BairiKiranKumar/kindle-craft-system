# Non-negotiable publishing policy (updated Oct 2026)

## Product integrity
- Original art only. Never reproduce exact compositions from a competitor's cover or interiors.
- No copyrighted franchise characters, logos, recognizable branded mascots or prompts 'in style of [living artist]'.
- Verify licensing of any third-party fonts, images, design assets, AI model/provider outputs, and any redistribution rights (particularly before Gumroad).
- Scan every image for nonsensical limbs, accidental text, odd cultural/stereotyped depictions, unsafe scenarios, excessive tiny details, gray fills, watermarks or near-duplication.
- Parents of children ages 3–5 expect simple, easy-to-color large shapes; no unsupported developmental claims.
- Avoid hundreds of shallow books. A coherent brand and genuinely differentiated product matters more.

## Accuracy & disclosure
- AI-generated images (cover or interior) must be disclosed to Amazon during setup. Generated text also must be disclosed where applicable. AI assistance that only improves human-created work has a different definition; consult KDP policy.
- Metadata author name and title must match cover; descriptive claims need factual support. Do not add unsupported bestseller numbers or review statements.
- Use available live KDP categories (up to 3) and up to 7 relevant keyword phrases. Don't keyword-stuff titles or include competitors' brand names.
- KDP paperback on Amazon.com is the first test. Amazon.in lacks direct KDP paperback distribution. No KDP 'kindle coloring ebook' shortcut.
- Coloring books are generally not low-content; exceptions exist. They are specifically excluded from KDP Expanded Distribution.

## Print quality
- Interior no bleed: exact trim 8.5x11, one page per PDF page; avoid content close to gutter; 300 effective DPI.
- Cover: single PDF with correct spine from actual page count and paper type + 0.125 inches bleed all outside edges; reserve bar code; no spine text under 79 pages.
- Build script rejects missing/low-resolution/unapproved assets. Human must inspect a full KDP Print Previewer and order a physical proof before launch.
- Failure after Amazon preview always overrides a local PASS.

## Security and publishing authority
- Do not auto-submit books; do not gather identity or banking information in chat/repository.
- Only the account owner completes KDP onboarding, tax/identity forms, and final publish action.
- No financial guarantees. Run a small experiment before buying ads.
