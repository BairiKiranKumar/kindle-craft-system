# Future Gumroad business: sell the process, not inflated promises

## When to launch this system as a product

First demonstrate a complete book, KDP publisher approval, full local QA and at least 1-2 measured experiments. Profits are not assured. Buyers should pay for a useful reproducible *workflow*, not a claim of passive income.

## Product package to sell (after validation)

**Kiran Kindle Craft System - Creator Edition** (working name)
- Portable AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, GEMINI.md, Cursor rules
- Theme briefs and prompt generator, research/metadata templates
- Tested local CLI with install guide, example assets cleared for redistribution
- PDF interior / wrap production scripts, QA gate, royalty estimate
- Real screenshots/screenshares of final tool outputs and sample book (with permission and redaction)
- KDP setup and troubleshooting guide, versioned release notes, policy updates

Suggested price experiments **$19 starter / $49 creator**, not sales predictions or market research. Test demand with a waitlist, small free demo and buyer feedback first. Avoid selling copyrighted bestseller covers/interiors, image generators' proprietary assets you cannot sublicense, real customer screenshots without permission, or licensed fonts.

## Gumroad checklist

1. Use https://gumroad.com to create a seller account. Enter payout/identity details on Gumroad itself, not into chat or this repo.
2. Create a **digital product**, give a truthful headline: `Kiran Kindle Craft System - Multi-Agent Coloring Book Publishing Toolkit`.
3. Offer a short license text (personal/commercial use of your original scripts/templates, but exclude third-party art and services; warranty disclaimers). If AI-generated code/art copyright status is material, get jurisdictional legal advice; do not promise exclusivity or guaranteed copyright.
4. Zip docs, source code, a safe example plus reproducible tests. Include operating system/install instructions, minimum Python version, support scope, change log, policy recheck date.
5. Upload screenshots, a walkthrough, demo files and a truthful description. Make no false income claims or affiliation claims with Amazon, Anthropic, Google, OpenAI or Gumroad.
6. Show an honest FAQ: no free image API calls, art generation may require separate subscriptions/credits, KDP account is free but cannot guarantee acceptance, user is responsible for originality, licensing, local taxes.
7. Start with a narrow audience: developers/designers and creatives comfortable with AI coding agents. Publish demos via @kiranbuildswithai only with proof.
