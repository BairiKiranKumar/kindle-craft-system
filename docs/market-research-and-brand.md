# Demand research, brand direction, and differentiation

## What we can actually conclude

As of the research for this kit (12 Oct 2026), KDP policy, royalties, onboarding and format specifications were verified via official KDP pages. A retrieved **Amazon children's coloring-book bestseller page was an older cached snapshot** (~2025, not an authenticated 2026 live chart): it listed themes such as bold-and-easy scenes, oceans, toddler 'first things', pencil-control activities and licensed cartoon content. These are **category signals only**, not proof of 2026 bestseller rank or demand. Don't publish the books from the cached list as 'current bestsellers'.

Selected reference: https://us.amazon.com/Best-Sellers-Books/zgbs/books/3374

### Working positioning comparisons

| Theme | Demand hypothesis | Competition risk | Differentiation test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Familiar animals, ages 3-5 | Evergreen, parent-friendly | High | Original animal characters + varied actual scenes, consistent simple line thickness |
| Birds around the world | Strong educational adjacency | Medium | Original colorful front cover + authentic species identities with optional labels in separate intro |
| Ocean life | Recognizable, visually fun | High | 50 different habitats/actions, not copy-paste fish pages |
| Dinosaurs with big outlines | High interest among children | High | Gentle friendly species with non-scary character personality, clear identification |
| Locally inspired Indian nature | Potential niche for India/international diaspora | Unknown | Distinct culturally grounded scenes with verified accuracy, but KDP doesn't print-direct to Amazon.in |

Do not interpret these as validated Amazon keyword volumes or confirmed sales. Price needs Amazon.com category research now and updated before launch.

## Competitor research template

Use `products/<slug>/research/competitive_scan.csv`. Collect at least 12–20 real competitor listing URLs. Verify paperback format, observed price, number of substantive illustrations (if visible), page count, review themes and title claim clarity. Record unknown when Amazon hides fields. Check current Amazon autocomplete, not guessed search-volume scores. Date each row.

## Proposed brand system (working, not cleared)

Brand working name: **Kiran Craft Kids**. Parent project/system name: **Kiran Kindle Craft System**. Target audience: parents and gift buyers of children aged 3–5. Values: warm, playful, simple, clean, original, child-appropriate, affordable, no clutter. Tone: clear, human, accessible. Avoid exaggerated 'IQ boosting', 'therapy' or developmental claims. Product visual direction: oversized objects, expressive animals, broad black contour lines, large fillable shapes, calm cream+peach+mint covers, consistent title typography, small age badge. Every theme should be recognizable as a series without cloning the previous book.

No brand/trademark ownership is asserted. Before publishing, check obvious collisions across Amazon Books, Google, domain/social handles, India's IP registry and U.S. USPTO trademark search; consult a professional if ambiguous. Do not use the branded name commercially until cleared.

## Pilot experiment and economic reality

At 52 pages, $9.99 Amazon.com US retail, B&W white large trim: KDP estimate **$3.15 royalty per copy** before income tax/withholding, ad spend, expenses. $9.99 is a **pricing hypothesis**; do not present as recommended by Amazon or actual competitor median.

- 20 copies: ~$63 pre-expense royalty.
- 100 copies: ~$315.
- 200 copies: ~$630.

Royalty is NOT profit after marketing, tax, paid art, software or labor. If ads cost more than royalty per incremental order, you lose money on those orders. The market may sell zero without strong discoverability.

Proof-of-demand launch: 1 finished book + 1 tested listing, 30-60 day reporting window. Record actual sales, average royalty, production expense and conversion insights (when Amazon reports it). Do not take a loan for a POD pilot. Only build another theme if you can explain how it differs meaningfully.
