# Realistic 30-day plan

Week 1: Set up KDP private legal/payout/tax details; finish competitor research (12-20 valid listings); validate brand name and title; create 50 scene briefs. Cap first experiment spending. Do NOT borrow.

Week 2: Produce 50 high-quality line artworks in batches of five with actual connected image tool; human review, revise, record rights. If the generator only gives ~1k pixel images, don't fake 300dpi by updating metadata or resampling. Change tools/workflow to output genuine high-resolution linework.

Week 3: Final metadata, build PDFs, inspect full 52-page interior, redesign cover to thumbnail-friendly, check barcode/spine using official KDP template, order physical proof. Time depends on delivery location.

Week 4: Correct print proof issues, publish only after quality review, monitor listing and produce 4 short educational showcase reels. No paid ads until conversion economics are understood.

Go/no-go after first 30-60 days: actual sales and customer signals vs full production cost. One well-finished product beats ten low-quality uploads. Income is uncertain and KDP pays with a delay.
