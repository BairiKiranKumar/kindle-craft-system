# KDP account setup + upload checklist (India resident, Oct 2026)

Kiran completes these steps privately at https://kdp.amazon.com . A coding agent must NEVER request or store PAN, bank account, ID scans, OTP, private home address, passwords or tax forms.

## Step 1 - Prepare
- Decide legal account holder: individual versus registered corporation. For a new personal publishing experiment, individual is commonly simplest; ask a tax professional if a formal business is planned.
- Keep legal name, phone, address, payment bank information and tax details handy **only for entering on Amazon**.
- Decide a display-name / pen-name separately from account legal identity; `Kiran Craft Kids` is a **working name only** and must be checked for trademark conflicts.
- Do not create duplicate KDP accounts to bypass limits or identity checks.

## Step 2 - Register
1. Go to https://kdp.amazon.com ; Sign in with an existing Amazon account or Sign Up with a new one.
2. At `Your Account`, select business type and enter your **legal** name/address, not your pen name.
3. Complete two-step verification.
4. Under `Getting Paid`, add a bank account using exactly the bank-registered holder name and required account/routing information.
5. Under `Tax Information`, complete KDP's online non-US tax questionnaire (normally a W-8 workflow). Enter applicable tax identification information privately. Don't guess a treaty rate or ask an AI agent to submit it.
6. If prompted, complete identity verification via Amazon's official flow. Supported ID types vary by country.
7. Confirm bank/tax/identity status in your KDP account.

Important: The default U.S. withholding described by KDP is 30% for relevant non-US-person royalties; treaty benefits can change this based on your actual verified tax profile. An Indian author may need Indian tax advice. KDP pays standard royalties around **60 days after the end of the sales month**, and additional days may apply for transfer to Indian accounts.

## Step 3 - Create paperback (only after final art + proof)
1. Bookshelf -> **+ Create** -> Paperback.
2. Details tab: language English, title/subtitle **exactly matching final cover**, author display name, description, 7 or fewer keywords, up to 3 genuinely relevant categories from current category picker, appropriate reading age if field exists.
3. Rights: select Worldwide only where you own worldwide rights. Identify AI-generated images accurately during the content form.
4. Content tab: request a **free KDP ISBN** if this book qualifies (coloring books generally do), black ink/white paper, **8.5 × 11 in**, **No Bleed**, and matte cover (or selected finish matching final file).
5. Upload `interior_8.5x11_no_bleed.pdf`; upload **one** `cover_full_wrap.pdf` containing back, spine, front. Do not upload front-only PDF.
6. Launch **Print Previewer**. Inspect EVERY spread, title, margins/gutter, page count, barcode and thin lines. Fix any errors.
7. Order a physical proof copy and inspect readability, cropped elements, line weight, margins, binding, image quality, title, paper and barcode placement.
8. Rights & Pricing: choose valid territory, primary marketplace Amazon.com, list price. Use KDP's **live royalty estimate**. **Do not opt into Expanded Distribution**, which specifically excludes coloring books.
9. Review all fields; only then press **Publish Your Paperback Book**.
10. After listing becomes live: record Amazon URL/ASIN, test description appearance, monitor royalties/refunds, and improve only based on real data.

If you change page count, **rebuild the wrap cover** because the white-paper spine changes.
