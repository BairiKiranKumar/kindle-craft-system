# Per-page visual QC rubric

Each art must score PASS in all categories, visually assessed by Kiran. The automated validator confirms dimensions/filename/status; it cannot establish legal rights or visual coherence.

1. Original scene with documented image-generation rights and no branded characters.
2. Correct animal/bird/dinosaur anatomy (appropriate stylization allowed); no missing feet, warped eyes or detached objects.
3. Black outlines are thick and continuous, white spaces are large enough for young children to color.
4. No clipped heads/feet/props. No distracting tiny patterns, grayscale shading, noise, gradients, watermarks, accidental text or border shadows.
5. Meaningfully different pose and setting from other pages, not simple recolors or rearrangements.
6. 300 effective DPI at print size; evaluate detail at actual 8.5x11 paper size.
7. No misrepresentation in captions or marketing (e.g., do not label whales as fish).
8. Cover should remain readable at a thumbnail size; barcode area should remain unobstructed.

Only after checking artwork and licensing may the owner set `approved` and `rights_checked` true for that page in `scenes.json`.
